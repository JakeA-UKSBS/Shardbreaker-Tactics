# Art credits

- **Raven Fantasy Icons** by [Clockwork Raven](https://clockworkraven.itch.io/raven-fantasy-icons) (free version). Used for relic, item, weapon, gear and tavern-meal icons in `art/relics/`, `art/icons/`, `art/meals/` and `art/ui/` (coin, mug). Commercial use allowed; the icons must not be redistributed or sold as an asset pack on their own, so only the individual icons the game uses are kept here. Exception: the Horn of Plenty relic (`art/relics/plenty.png`) is an original horn drawn for the game.
- **Pixel Isometric Tiles** by [crabling](https://scrabling.itch.io/pixel-isometric-tiles) (CC BY 4.0). Used for the boulder prop (`art/tiles/boulder.png`) and the water and ice ground (`art/tiles/water.png`, `art/tiles/ice.png`, the top faces flattened into square textures). Only the tiles the game uses are kept here.
- Campfire (`art/nodes/rest.png`), chests (`art/nodes/treasure.png`, `art/scenes/chest.png`) and the Bog Witch (`art/scenes/witch.png`) drawn by Rhi. Also the Hollow Bear and Krondis bosses (`art/bosses/hollow-bear.png`, `art/bosses/krondis.png`) the Bog Witch's hut and cauldron (`art/scenes/witch-hut.png`, `art/scenes/cauldron.png`), the Fallen Star's meteor (`art/scenes/meteor.png`) and the tankard on the node map (`art/nodes/tavern.png`).
- **Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup** tiles ([crawl/crawl](https://github.com/crawl/crawl), CC0). Rook's dagger is its `item/weapon/dagger.png`, stored as pixel data in `WPN_ART` in `js/09b-battle-rig.js`.
- **Dark Ages UI - Pixel Art Asset Pack** v1.0 by [Hypnobius](https://hypnobius.itch.io/dark-ages-ui). Used for the ornate box frame (`art/ui/frame.png`), the node map's scroll thumbs (`art/ui/scroll-v.png`, `scroll-h.png`) and the Ascension arrows (`art/ui/arrow-l.png`, `arrow-r.png`, cut from the ends of the horizontal bar). Only the pieces the game uses are kept here.
- Sunless Isle rocks, rubble, skulls, dead tree and crystal (`art/props/sunless-*.png`), the Healing Fountain's castle courtyard (`art/scenes/fountain.png`, from Free Castle Interior Pixel Game Backgrounds) and the Caged Traveller's abandoned village (`art/scenes/village.png`, from Free Pixel Art Abandoned Places Background Collection) from [CraftPix](https://craftpix.net) ([licence](https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/): free for commercial use in games, no credit required; the art must not be resold or redistributed as an asset pack, so only the pieces the game uses are kept here).
- **Free Medieval & Dungeon Pixel Art Asset Bundle** by [pulsarcp](https://itch.io/profile/pulsarcp) (free for any use, including commercial; no credit required). Used for the event props `art/scenes/well.png`, `anvil.png` (the Smith and the Old King's Forge), `altar.png` (Shrine of Trial, the Bleeding Seam), `desk.png` (Ruined Library) and `potions.png` (the Enchanter's Study), plus `stained-glass.png` and `banner.png` (the Wayside Chapel) and `red-floor.png` (the Empty Throne). `art/spare/` holds two more picks (a broken pillar and a chained wall) kept for future events.
- **Pixel Art Top Down - Basic** by [Cainos](https://cainos.itch.io/pixel-art-top-down-basic) (free for commercial use, credit appreciated but not required; must not be redistributed or resold as a pack, so only the pieces the game uses are kept here). `art/spare/platform.png` (round stone platform) and `art/spare/statue.png` (praying statue) are kept for future events.
- **Free Rocks Pixel Art Asset Pack** from [CraftPix](https://craftpix.net/freebies/free-rocks-pixel-art-asset-pack/) (same CraftPix licence as above). Used for the region boulders `art/props/sand-rock.png`, `ice-block.png`, `moss-rock.png`, `snow-peaks.png` and `red-spire.png` (padded to square). Only the rocks the game uses are kept here.
- **Pixel Combat** sound effects by Helton Yan. Trimmed to MP3 in `sfx/`: healing (`healing-gusts.mp3`), sigil attacks (`sparkle-zap.mp3`), Krondis's attack (`sand-swipe.mp3`), level-ups (`aura-up.mp3`), footsteps (`boots-grass.mp3`), weapon crits (`sword-crit.mp3`), and `firewall.mp3` kept for a future skill.

# Art credits

- **Raven Fantasy Icons** by [Clockwork Raven](https://clockworkraven.itch.io/raven-fantasy-icons) (free version). Used for relic, item, weapon, gear and tavern-meal icons in `art/relics/`, `art/icons/`, `art/meals/` and `art/ui/` (coin, mug). Commercial use allowed; the icons must not be redistributed or sold as an asset pack on their own, so only the individual icons the game uses are kept here.
- **Pixel Isometric Tiles** by [crabling](https://scrabling.itch.io/pixel-isometric-tiles) (CC BY 4.0). Used for the boulder prop (`art/tiles/boulder.png`) and the water and ice ground (`art/tiles/water.png`, `art/tiles/ice.png`, the top faces flattened into square textures). Only the tiles the game uses are kept here.
- Campfire (`art/nodes/rest.png`) and chest (`art/nodes/treasure.png`, `art/scenes/chest.png`) drawn by Rhi. Also the Hollow Bear and Krondis bosses (`art/bosses/hollow-bear.png`, `art/bosses/krondis.png`) and the Bog Witch's hut (`art/scenes/witch-hut.png`).
- **Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup** tiles ([crawl/crawl](https://github.com/crawl/crawl), CC0). Rook's dagger is its `item/weapon/dagger.png`, stored as pixel data in `WPN_ART` in `js/09b-battle-rig.js`.

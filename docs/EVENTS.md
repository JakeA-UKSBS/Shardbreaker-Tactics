# Events

- Events (`EVENTS`, `js/20-events.js`) don't repeat within a run while fresh ones are left. They open a full-screen scene drawn for that event (`eventScene()`, tinted to the region), with choices underneath and the outcome shown in the same box: Wandering Smith, Abandoned Armoury, Blood Shrine, Training Ground, Travelling Merchant (3 items for gold), Gambler's Table (bet 30 for 60), The Golden Idol (rare relic for −2 max HP all round), The Caged Traveller (free unit), Healing Fountain (+3 max HP for one, or +1 Res for all), Wishing Well (25 gold for +1 Luck all round), Old Battlefield (search for gear at a risk, or +20 EXP).
- More events: Storm Altar (only with Corvin in the squad; the Thunderhead Sigil is his), Specialist's Cache (crates matched to your squad: Longbow if you have an archer, Throwing Knives for a Rogue, Far Charm for a Mender, Spark Sigil for a caster, Rune Staff for a Summoner), Old Veteran, Statue of Valour, Drill Sergeant, Toll Bridge, Bog Witch, Strange Fog, Suspicious Chest, Ruined Library.
- Act 2 only (`act:2`): Fallen Star (Meteor Sigil), Storm Spire (Heaven's Bolt or Far Charm), The Oracle, Mountain Sage, Dragon's Hoard.
- Woes (`WOES`, `run.woes`): bad luck from events, shown in red on the map and counted down after each won fight. Wounded (every unit starts the next fight 5 HP down), Weary Legs (−1 Move), Marked (one unit acts as if it had Taunt).
